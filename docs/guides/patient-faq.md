# Patient FAQ

**How do I book?** Open the app (or the link from the clinic) → Book → choose who the appointment is for, the doctor, mode, date and time.

**Do I have to pay in advance?** Online consultations are paid at booking. For clinic visits, the clinic decides; you can also pay after the visit by the link sent on WhatsApp/SMS.

**Which payment methods work?** UPI (GPay, PhonePe, Paytm and others), cards, net banking and wallets, processed securely by Cashfree. We never see your card or UPI PIN.

**I paid but it shows unpaid.** It usually updates within a minute. If not, contact the clinic with your payment reference; any failed payment is normally returned by your bank.

**Where is my prescription?** In the app under Prescriptions, and as a link on WhatsApp/SMS. Tap **View or share PDF** to open the signed copy in Files, Books, a PDF viewer or a chat app; revised and cancelled versions open too and say so. It is digitally signed; pharmacies can scan the QR code to check it. The app keeps no copy once you close the share sheet (on Android, once you open another PDF) or sign out.

**Where do I upload my test report?** In the app under Tests, which lists the tests your doctor ordered for you and your family. Tap **Upload result** on the test and choose the report, a PDF or a photo (JPEG, PNG, HEIC or HEIF) of up to 25 MB; your clinic may accept fewer file types or set a lower size limit, and the app tells you if it does. You can add up to 10 results to a test until the doctor has reviewed it; after that a new result needs a new test. Tap **View result** to open a report in the share sheet. The app keeps no copy of a report it opened once you close the share sheet (on Android, as soon as you start opening another result, even if that fails) or sign out, and the copy of a file you chose to upload is deleted as soon as it is sent, or refused, and again on sign-out.

**Can I manage my family's records?** Yes. Everyone the clinic registered on your mobile number appears in the app when you sign in with it, with the children they look after. Add others under Family: adults who share your number, or someone you look after, such as a child or an elderly parent. Children's records are managed by their guardian.

**Can I take back my consent?** Yes. Under Privacy and consents you can withdraw the health-data consent at any time; the app then stops showing health records until you accept it again. The clinic keeps the records it needs by law.

**What is ABHA?** Your Ayushman Bharat Health Account. Linking it lets you share records between hospitals with your consent. It is optional.

**Is the doctor recording me?** Only if you agree. The doctor may ask to use an AI assistant to write notes; you can say no, and you can ask to stop at any time.

**How do I stop messages?** Reply STOP on WhatsApp or change preferences in Profile. Important messages (login codes, prescriptions) cannot be turned off.

**How do I delete my account?** Profile → Data and privacy → Delete account. Medical records the doctor created are kept for the period required by law.
