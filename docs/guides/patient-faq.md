# Patient FAQ

**Can I use a web browser instead of the app?** Yes. Open the patient portal at the web address your clinic gives you, which ends in `/app/login` (the clinic's link fills in its clinic ID for you; otherwise type the ID, for example `demo-clinic`). Enter your mobile number, tap **Send code** and type the 6-digit code from the SMS. The portal is made for up-to-date browsers on phones and computers. The first time, tick the privacy notice, the terms of use and the health-data consent, then **Accept and continue**. Use **हिंदी** or **English** at the top to change the language. The web portal has everything the app has: your appointments, booking, prescriptions, tests and results, family, and privacy and consents.

**How do I book?** Open the app or the web portal (or the link from the clinic) → Book → choose who the appointment is for, the doctor, the clinic, the date and time, and add a reason if you like. You see the fee (paid at the clinic) and how late you can still cancel online.

**Will I stay signed in?** Only if you choose. When you type the code, **Keep me signed in on this device** is off: you are signed out when you close the browser, after 15 minutes without use (in every tab of the portal), and when you tap **Sign out**, which signs out every tab. Tick it only on your own phone or computer; you then stay signed in for up to 30 days until you sign out. On a shared computer, always tap **Sign out** when you finish.

**How do I cancel?** Under Appointments, tap **Cancel appointment** on an upcoming visit and confirm. Online cancelling closes a set time before the visit (the booking page says how long); after that, call the clinic.

**Do I have to pay in advance?** Online consultations are paid at booking. For clinic visits, the clinic decides; you can also pay after the visit by the link sent on WhatsApp/SMS.

**Which payment methods work?** UPI (GPay, PhonePe, Paytm and others), cards, net banking and wallets, processed securely by Cashfree. We never see your card or UPI PIN.

**I paid but it shows unpaid.** It usually updates within a minute. If not, contact the clinic with your payment reference; any failed payment is normally returned by your bank.

**Where is my prescription?** In the app under Prescriptions, and as a link on WhatsApp/SMS. Tap **View or share PDF** to open the signed copy in Files, Books, a PDF viewer or a chat app; revised and cancelled versions open too and say so. It is digitally signed; pharmacies can scan the QR code to check it. The app keeps no copy once you close the share sheet (on Android, once you open another PDF) or sign out. On the web portal it is under **Prescriptions** too: **Open PDF** shows the signed copy in a new tab, and **Download PDF** saves it, named after the prescription number and version. If your browser blocks new tabs, the page shows a link, **Your browser blocked the new tab. Download the file instead.**; nothing is downloaded until you click it. Signing out (or being signed out after 15 minutes) closes the tabs the portal opened. A downloaded file stays in your downloads, so on a shared computer delete it when you are done.

**Where do I upload my test report?** In the app under Tests, which lists the tests your doctor ordered for you and your family. Tap **Upload result** on the test and choose the report, a PDF or a photo (JPEG, PNG, HEIC or HEIF) of up to 25 MB; your clinic may accept fewer file types or set a lower size limit, and the app tells you if it does. You can add up to 10 results to a test until the doctor has reviewed it; after that a new result needs a new test. Tap **View result** to open a report in the share sheet. The app keeps no copy of a report it opened once you close the share sheet (on Android, as soon as you start opening another result, even if that fails) or sign out, and the copy of a file you chose to upload is deleted as soon as it is sent, or refused, and again on sign-out. On the web portal, open **Tests**, click **Upload result** on the test and choose the file from your phone or computer; the same types, sizes and limits apply, and the page says why a file was refused. **View result** opens a PDF, JPEG or PNG report in a new tab; for HEIC and HEIF photos, which most browsers cannot show, the button reads **Download result 1** (2, 3 and so on) and saves the photo as `test-result-<n>.heic` (or `.heif`). As with prescriptions, a blocked tab gives you a download link instead, and signing out closes the tabs.

**Where are the sheets and reports my doctor gave me?** On the web portal under **Documents**: exercise sheets, diet charts, reports and other documents your doctor shared with you and the family members on your account, newest first, with the doctor's note. New ones are marked **New** until someone on the account opens them. **View** opens the document in a new tab (and the browser's download button saves it). If your doctor withdraws a document (for example one sent by mistake), it no longer shows. They do not come on WhatsApp or SMS yet, and the Patient app does not show them yet.

**Can I manage my family's records?** Yes. Everyone the clinic registered on your mobile number appears in the app when you sign in with it, with the children they look after. Add others under Family, in the app or the web portal: adults who share your number, or someone you look after, such as a child or an elderly parent (choose yourself under **Who looks after them**). Children's records are managed by their guardian.

**Can I take back my consent?** Yes. Under Privacy and consents, in the app or on the web portal (from its home page), you see what you accepted and when, and you can withdraw the health-data consent at any time; after you confirm, the app and the portal stop showing health records until you accept it again. The clinic keeps the records it needs by law.

**What is ABHA?** Your Ayushman Bharat Health Account. Linking it lets you share records between hospitals with your consent. It is optional.

**Is the doctor recording me?** Only if you agree. The doctor may ask to use an AI assistant to write notes; you can say no, and you can ask to stop at any time.

**How do I stop messages?** Reply STOP on WhatsApp or change preferences in Profile. Important messages (login codes, prescriptions) cannot be turned off.

**How do I delete my account?** Profile → Data and privacy → Delete account. Medical records the doctor created are kept for the period required by law.
