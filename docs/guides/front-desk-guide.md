# Front Desk Guide

For reception staff using the web dashboard (or the Clinic app on a tablet).

## Start of day
Open the queue for today; check the doctor's sessions; turn on the waiting-room display.

## Patients arriving
- **Booked patient:** find in Booked → **Check in** → token issued.
- **Walk-in:** search by phone or UHID → existing or quick register → token.
- **Scan & Share:** patient scans the clinic QR → appears in the queue with details → check in.
- Record vitals if asked (weight is required for children under 12).

## Bookings by phone
Use **Book for patient**; the patient gets a WhatsApp/SMS confirmation. You can overbook within the clinic's limit.

## Billing
- Create the bill (consultation + items, discount).
- **Send payment link** (WhatsApp, SMS fallback) or record a **counter payment** (cash, clinic UPI QR, card machine).
- Receipts are sent automatically when paid. Refunds need a reason.

## When things change
- Doctor running late → **Broadcast delay**.
- Session cancelled → **Bulk reschedule**; patients are notified.
- WhatsApp replies from patients → **Inbox** (reply within the 24-hour window).
- Patient lost their old phone number → verify photo ID in person, then **Change number**.

## Privacy
You can see appointments, contact details and bills, not clinical notes. Never share patient details over personal phones or chats.
