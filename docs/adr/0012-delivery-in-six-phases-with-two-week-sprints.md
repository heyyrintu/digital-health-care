# ADR 0012: Delivery in six phases with two-week sprints

- **Status:** Accepted
- **Date:** 2026-10-06
- **Deciders:** Tech lead, platform owner
- **Related:** Build Plan decision D12

## Context
Scope is large; web unblocks the clinic early while mobile and integrations follow.

## Options considered
| Option | For | Against |
|---|---|---|
| Sequential web, then mobile, then integrations | Simple | 10+ months |
| Overlapping phases | About 7 months to pilot | Needs a full team and tight coordination |

## Decision
Phases 0–6 per Build Plan section 9 with overlap of phases 1–3; sprint demos to the doctor every second Friday.

## Consequences
- Smaller team removes overlap and adds 2–3 months.

## Review trigger
Hiring delays or scope change.
